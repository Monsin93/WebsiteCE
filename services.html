How it works

From first call to final report

  1. Scoping call

    A free 30-minute call to define objectives, samples and deliverables.

  2. Quotation

    A formal quotation with scope, timeline and price.

  3. Agreement & NDA

    Service agreement and confidentiality terms signed before any data or samples move.

  4. Project work

    Your project lead shares progress at agreed milestones.

  5. Report delivery

    Full report, raw data and figures, with a debrief call to discuss results.

Pricing & taxes

List prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT, withholding and other taxes, which apply according to your location. Shipping and bank charges are borne by the client. Quotations are valid for 60 days.

Confidentiality & IP

Your structures, samples and data stay confidential. Results from fee-for-service work belong to you. Collaborative research follows Chulalongkorn University IP regulations.

Samples & timelines

Turnaround is counted from receipt of complete samples or data and a confirmed order. Sample requirements and shipping instructions come with your quotation.
